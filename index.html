About Sonali

A Voice That
Tells Stories

Sonali is a passionate and seasoned singer, songwriter, musician and entertainer with a heart full of stories and melodies.

Born and raised in the vibrant island nation of Sri Lanka, Sonali’s passion for music began at a young age and has since blossomed into a dynamic, international career spanning continents and cultures. With her soulful voice, heartfelt lyrics, and a deep connection to her roots, Sonali has been captivating audiences for over a decade.
